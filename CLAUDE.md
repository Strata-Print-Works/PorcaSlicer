# Porca Slicer

Porca is our Orca desktop fork for preparing work across multiple independently configured printer plates. Preserve the mature slicer and native controls, improve the workspace and printer workflow incrementally, and follow the approved Monitor first layout. The earlier paid standalone MultiSlicer architecture is not Porca's product direction.

@AGENTS.md

`AGENTS.md` is the shared source of truth for product goals, UX copy, compatibility, engineering and verification. Read and follow it before making changes. Keep Porca and other suspect apps closed and use offline verification only while the user's current Wi-Fi testing restriction remains in force.
