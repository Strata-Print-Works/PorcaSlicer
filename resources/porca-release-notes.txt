Opening a recent project from Home has been reported to close Porca unexpectedly. This is under investigation.

When plates open scattered across the workspace, right-click a plate and choose Arrange plates. Movable beds form tidy rows, and models keep their places on each bed. Locked plates and plates in a stage stay where you put them.

To make better use of the space on each bed, right-click the workspace or a stage and choose Optimize plates. Review the proposed placements before applying them. Objects stay on their current plates and keep the same face on the bed; plates without a complete layout stay as they are.

Projects can now hold up to 72 plates, including plates added by importing another project.

Each plate keeps its own printer setup while you move between jobs. Save a Porca project to keep plate positions and setups for the next time you open it.
